UT LECTURES PLUS

UT Lectures Plus

A lecture captioning extension developed for UT Austin's lecture video recording systems, LecturesOnline and TowerLA.

Timeline
Fall 2024 - Present
Team
3 Product Managers, 5 Designers
Tools
Figma, Chrome Extensions API, User Research

Problem

UT Lectures Plus required a redesign to better reflect its purpose as an accessibility-focused extension giving students greater control over how they study. Research surfaced a clear pattern: students were abandoning lectures they couldn't quickly navigate back into.

My Role

I led a 3-person product management team and a 5-person design team through a complete overhaul of our studying extension. I used research findings to set priorities for the design team, then worked with them directly to translate insights into the redesign.

Lecture Landing Page

I identified two core problems: students couldn't tell what content a lecture covered, and the naming convention was hard to parse. I directed the design team through a fix, adding custom lecture titles and replacing the list view with video previews.

after

Video Player

Testing showed over 75% of users left the default settings to make manual adjustments—a strong signal the interface wasn't meeting their needs out of the box. I led the design team to add custom captions, automatic chaptering, and bookmarking. Caption customization, which was a novel feature I introduced, became a key feature students used regularly.

before
after

Outcome

In testing, users located the correct lecture two minutes faster than with the original design. Following the release, downloads doubled and user engagement increased significantly.

CSA CRIMSON

CSA Crimson

A beginner-friendly dance team where students learn and film dances taught and produced by other students.

Timeline
Fall 2023 - Present
Team
Co-founder with 5 other students
Tools
Production, Choreography, Community Management

Overview

I lead the entire filming process from planning through post-production, releasing a new video every month following a strict one-month timeline from teaching to release.

Teaching

Before I begin organizing dancers, I select several song options for our team members to choose from, and it goes to a vote. Once the song is decided, I schedule our practices over the next two weeks.

Filming

First, I reach out to videographers to find availability and a filming location. Second, I coordinate with our 7-8 dancers to find the best time, taking into account their schedules and the time of day I want to film.

Storyboarding

Once filming is scheduled, I focus on filming logistics, particularly storyboarding the final cover. I provide the videographer with a storyboard, a shot list, and reference videos, giving them a clear picture of the angles and shots I want at each point in the song.

Post-Production

After filming day, I personally edit each video. From over a hundred clips, I select the shot that best fits each section of the song, choosing takes that match the final vision and can be cut together into one cohesive video.

Release

I oversee the release process, making sure our editing team works quickly and closely with our publicity team so each video goes out on time, with a coordinated social media post, tracking deadlines to keep a consistent monthly release schedule.

Outcome

I track the success of each video and the satisfaction of each dancer via surveys. I use this information to provide a wider variety of opportunities to the team, ensuring 100% of our team members are able to participate and enjoy their experience.

NATO × WMGIC

NATO × WMGIC Competition

Competitors were asked to design a solution to disinformation within a category such as AI, Clean Energy, Public Health, or Terrorism.

Timeline
Fall 2024
Team
4-person team, 2-hour time limit
Tools
Strategic Planning, Policy Analysis, Cost-Benefit Analysis

Problem

AI-created bots and deepfakes were playing a gradually expanding role in foreign actors' disinformation campaigns. I had to find a solution for NATO to combat the proliferation of disinformation online.

Process

Working within a strict two-hour time limit, I adapted NATO's existing Locked Shields cyber defense exercises to the disinformation problem, allowing my team to move efficiently in the competition without sacrificing rigor.

Solution Framework

From this foundation, I identified two core requirements and structured the solution around them. The first was a training exercise pairing member states sharing best practices against disinformation with a designated red team generating AI-driven disinformation, giving participants firsthand experience with both sides of the problem.

Public Education Strategy

The second requirement was a public-facing training initiative, incorporating social media campaigns and gamified tools, equipping member countries to run a coordinated public education strategy on identifying disinformation.

Results

The result was a complete submission including an implementation plan, cost-benefit analysis, and sustainability analysis, built around a low-cost, scalable solution adapted from an existing NATO framework and delivered within our two-hour constraint. Grand prize: $500.
